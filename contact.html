Before you reach out

Quick scheduling answers

Do I need a referral?

No referral is required to request routine mobile foot care. Kirsten may recommend follow-up with a podiatrist or physician if she observes a concern outside the scope of the visit.

Can I schedule for a parent or loved one?

Yes. Family members and caregivers regularly arrange visits. Please make sure the client or their authorized decision-maker agrees to the appointment.

Can our senior community host a clinic?

Yes. Clinic-style pricing is available for three or more clients at one location. Contact Kirsten to discuss space, scheduling, and recurring visit options.
