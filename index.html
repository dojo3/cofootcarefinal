Who is mobile foot care for?

Home visits are a good fit for seniors, people with limited mobility, adults who cannot safely reach or manage their feet, and clients who simply prefer care at home.

What does a foot care visit cost?

A private home visit is $85 per person and typically lasts about 30 minutes. Two clients at one location are $75 each; clinic-style visits for three or more clients are $50 each. A $10 trip fee may apply outside the core service area.

Do you serve assisted living communities?

Yes. Kirsten visits private homes, assisted living centers, senior communities, and group homes. Contact her to discuss an individual visit or a recurring group clinic.

Is this a replacement for a podiatrist?

No. Mobile foot care supports routine nail and skin care and does not replace diagnosis or treatment by a physician or podiatrist. Kirsten refers clients when a concern needs additional evaluation.
