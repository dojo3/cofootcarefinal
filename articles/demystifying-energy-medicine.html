Healing arts

Demystifying Energy Medicine

By Kirsten Antony, RNSeptember 30, 2017 · 3 min read

Originally published in Prime Time News

Browse the collection

Many people adhere to a Newtonian worldview and to the tenet that seeing is believing. Quantum Physics is turning that belief flipside and shows us that believing is seeing. Studies show that on a quantum level, an observer can affect what is reality. In other words, consciousness creates reality. This premise is what is known as the Quantum Theory. I am not a physicist, but I do apply many types of energy work in my life. I would like to illuminate a few basic principles of quantum physics and how that applies to energy work. I would like to take you on a journey from what some perceive as “woo woo” to an “a-ha” moment.

The whole universe is made of energy. We are made of energy. Anything made of matter is made entirely of energy. Anything visible to the eye is made up of matter which is 99.99% space. What is in that space? The quantum field. On the sub-atomic level, matter is composed of energy in the form of waves and vibrations. The quanta particles are what can change by means of human observation. Energy workers can use the quantum field and through observation and intention can facilitate a healing state in which the human body can experience change.

This process of change may be occurring though quantum entanglement. Quantum entanglement shows us that two quantum particles that are entangled can instantaneously exchange information outside of time and space. I believe that it is in this wherein distance healing, spontaneous healing and miracles can take place. This concept is what Albert Einstein is quoted as calling “spooky action at a distance”. Einstein was bothered by these principles and physicists today are still perplexed by parts of current quantum mechanics. This is the proverbial rabbit hole and I think we have just begun the jump within.

This web of energy that connects us has been known throughout the ages by mystics and healers. It is through this information matrix that our cells can be affected. The quantum wave function can be changed by observation. The quantum field can be changed by thoughts. By positive energy vibrations, the cells in the body can be positively influenced by this healing energy.

There are an abundance of energy therapies practiced. Some energy therapies are an ancient healing art and originate from various parts of the world. There is such a variety of healing therapies and many use different methods to achieve a healing effect. Some energy therapies include: Craniosacral Therapy, Healing Touch, Quantum Field Healing, Reflexology, Qigong, Yoga and Accupuncture.
You do not have to be a scientist or a mystic to understand that we all can be facilitators to healing and apply energy medicine in our own lives. Holding someone’s hand in a caring way, offering a listening ear, speaking words of appreciation, random acts of kindness, and living a life with your whole heart open are all practices within our grasp. Love is the greatest healer of all.
